## Executive decision

Go to market with one neutral commercial identity, one retained specialist
surface, and offer-specific paths:

1. **Converspan** becomes the working commercial parent for conversation that
   turns into visible, retained work.
2. **SFDC24** remains the existing Salesforce acquisition surface while its
   trademark and affiliation position is resolved. It is not the parent brand
   and receives no new paid brand investment.
3. Website prototyping, applied-AI learning, language practice, shopping
   research, and home research begin as routes or controlled experiments under
   Converspan. A vertical receives its own domain only after it demonstrates a
   distinct buyer, acquisition route, repeatable delivery, and positive
   contribution.
4. Spend is released in small, reversible stages. Domain protection is the
   first small stage. Paid acquisition is not released during the first 30
   days.

This deliberately chooses focus over a collection of speculative microsites.
The technology can support many miniboards; the commercial portfolio should
only create a new public brand when the market earns it.

### Clearance boundary

Direct exact-term searches of the official Canadian Intellectual Property
Office trademark field returned no result for Converspan, Page and Mark,
Step and Ship, FluentSpan, Rig and Rank, or Home and Why. A positive-control
Salesforce query returned records, demonstrating that the search was
functioning. The CIPO page reported its database updated 2026-09-23.

This is a knockout screen, not a legal opinion. Direct USPTO and WIPO searches
were not completed because their public interfaces did not return usable
results in this review. Common-law use, confusing similarity, corporate-name
availability, and class-specific rights remain separate questions.

`Blackboard` remains the internal control-plane name and is not commercialized
as the public brand. `SFDC24` is retained operationally, not declared cleared.
Salesforce's published guidelines restrict incorporation of its marks or
confusing abbreviations into domain names and source identifiers without
permission. The safe commercial destination is the neutral Converspan seller
identity.

## Capacity and kill rules

- Hard owner-capacity ceiling: **24 hours/week**.
- Sold delivery ceiling: **20 hours/week**; preserve at least 4 hours for sales,
  administration, QA, and recovery.
- Stop selling when sold-but-undelivered backlog reaches two weeks.
- Pause an offer if delivery exceeds its cap by more than 25% twice, measured
  contribution is negative twice, a material complaint or data incident occurs,
  or 20 qualified conversations produce no sale.
- Customer credentials are not stored in general agent context. Salesforce
  work is authorized, sandbox-first, least-privilege, and production writes
  require a separately approved scope.
- Do not market continuous audio, live design generation, autonomous Salesforce
  modification, or another capability until exact end-to-end evidence exists.
