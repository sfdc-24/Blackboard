Three frontier models agreeing is being treated as validation. I think it may be
shared training bias. "Niche down, sell a diagnostic, don't chase shiny objects,
lead with the client's pain" is the single most over-represented opinion in
business-advice text on the internet. Three models trained largely on that text
agreeing with it is not evidence — it is an echo.

So assume all five conclusions above are wrong *for the same reason*: they are
